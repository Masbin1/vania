Rumus
Proration = active_days / 30   (full month = 1.0)
Base Share = branch_target × (FTE / total_FTE)
Incentive Target = base_share × proration + carry_forward
Vacant Pool = Σ vacant_slots + Σ base_share × (1 − proration)
Bonus = pool × (FTEreceiver / Σ FTEfull-month)
Langkah Perhitungan
1
Hitung base share per orang
Lead: 4.500.000 × 1.5 / 4.5 = Rp 1.500.000
Member A: 4.500.000 × 1.0 / 4.5 = Rp 1.000.000
Member B: 4.500.000 × 1.0 / 4.5 = Rp 1.000.000
Member C: 4.500.000 × 1.0 / 4.5 = Rp 1.000.000
2
Prorate target si resign (Member C)
Resign 21 Aug → aktif 21 hari.
Proration = 21 / 30 = 0.7000
Incentive target = 1.000.000 × 0.7 = Rp 700.000
3
Hitung vacant pool
Sisa yang tidak di-cover Member C:
1.000.000 × (1 − 0.7) = Rp 300.000
Tidak ada slot kosong (vacant), jadi pool = Rp 300.000.
4
Distribusi pool → bonus (hanya yang full sebulan)
Penerima: Lead (1.5) + Member A (1.0) + Member B (1.0) = 3.5 FTE

Lead: 300.000 × 1.5 / 3.5 = Rp 128.571
Member A: 300.000 × 1.0 / 3.5 = Rp 85.714
Member B: 300.000 × 1.0 / 3.5 = Rp 85.714
Member C: Rp 0 (resign, dikecualikan dari pool)
